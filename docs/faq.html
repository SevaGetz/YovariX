FAQ

Can I get banned for using YovariX?

Client modifications are against X's Terms of Service, and that includes YovariX. All built-in plugins are harmless and you shouldn't get banned for using them.

Still, if losing your account would be a disaster, it's safest not to use any client mods at all. And don't post screenshots with YovariX where you might get reported.

Which browsers does it support?

Any browser with a userscript manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey.

How do updates work?

YovariX checks for a new version when X opens, at most once every two hours. You can also check manually in the settings.
